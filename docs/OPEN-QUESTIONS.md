36. **Dashboard: spreading monthly costs.** Rent and salaries count on the day they are entered, so a single day can show a loss. Do we want an accrual option (spread a monthly expense across its days), a "recurring expense" feature, or is "This month" view enough? Also: should prime cost (food + labour) get its own card once salaries have a fixed category?

37. **QR menu.** *Answered by the owner:* one QR for the whole restaurant (no per-table codes); dish photos, descriptions, halal/vegetarian/spicy labels and allergens are wanted; items can be hidden from guests. *Still open:* (a) which address the QR carries: the till's LAN address (works only on the restaurant Wi-Fi) or a website/domain (needs the menu online)? (b) the allergen wording is the owner's responsibility: who fills it in and checks it? (c) should CSV import/export carry the guest fields?

38. **Automatic daily report.** Today the owner taps a WhatsApp link to send the day's summary. For a message that goes out by itself at closing time we need a provider (WhatsApp Business API, SMS gateway or email), its credentials and the owner's consent. Which one, and should it also send alerts (cash short, large discount, printer offline)?

39. **ANSWERED: the print helper, see ADR 0029** (Windows only for now; to be tried on a real iTech IT800). **Printing from the cloud deployment.** Vercel cannot reach a restaurant's LAN thermal printer. *Answered by the owner:* first print from the browser (a receipt and a kitchen ticket page that opens the print dialog), later a small **local print agent** on a restaurant computer that fetches jobs from the cloud and prints to the thermal printer. Open: which browsers/printers must the browser path support (80 mm roll via the system print dialog, or WebUSB/Web Serial ESC/POS), and who installs the agent?

40. **Live updates on serverless.** The change stream lives in one process's memory; Vercel runs many instances, so it is switched off (`LIVE_EVENTS=off`) and screens poll every 8 s or more. A real push needs a shared channel (Redis pub/sub such as a Vercel Marketplace store, or a hosted realtime service). Decide when the number of devices makes polling too costly or too slow.

41. **Pricing and billing of restaurants.** `tenants.plan` is a free text label today; nothing meters or invoices. Decide: flat monthly fee, per device, per bill; who collects it; what happens to a restaurant that does not pay (the platform admin can already *Switch off* with a reason).

42. **Platform admin and a restaurant's data.** *Answered by the owner:* the platform admin only manages (create, switch off/on, archive, reset the owner's password, technical health) and **cannot see** sales, customers or menu. Open: do you want an audited, time-limited **support access** (for example 30 minutes, visible to the restaurant) for help desk cases? Also: who may have a platform admin account, and is a second factor required?

43. **Data protection for other people's restaurants.** Once the system holds another business's customers (khata names and phone numbers) the owner of the platform is a data processor: a written agreement with each restaurant, where the data is stored (Neon region), backup/retention period, what happens to the data when a restaurant leaves (archive keeps it; deletion is manual). Needs a lawyer, not code.

44. **Which payment methods count as "digital" for tax?** The Payments screen (Admin, Payments) offers Card, JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast/bank QR as *Digital*, and Cash and Khata as *Cash / other*. Once tax is switched on, a bill paid ONLY by digital methods uses the digital rate (the rule that was seeded for Punjab: digital 8%, other 16%). Nothing changes while tax is off. An accountant must confirm, per method and per province, which of these qualify (for example: is a card swiped on the restaurant's own bank machine "digital"? is a Raast QR to a personal account?). A restaurant can change a method's type in its Edit dialog, so the answer can be applied without code. Until answered, keep tax off in production.

45. **DECIDED by the owner: option (b), see ADR 0026** (still to be confirmed with the accountant: whether a kitchen order taken offline has any fiscal meaning before it is billed; billing itself is never offline). **Should a till keep taking orders while the internet is down?** Today (ADR 0025) the app *opens* offline and says clearly that nothing can be saved, because ARCHITECTURE decision 13 says the server is the only source of truth and writes are never queued. Queuing orders on the device and sending them later is possible, but it reverses that decision and raises real questions: bill numbers and tax are assigned by the server at settlement, a fiscal number cannot be obtained offline (see #6 and #34), and two tills editing the same table could clash. Options: (a) keep it as is; (b) queue only *adding items and sending to the kitchen* (never payments), settle when back; (c) the restaurant runs the LAN server on its own premises (the till keeps working with no internet at all, which is how the local install already behaves). Which fits your restaurants? The owner and the accountant should decide.

46. **Staff, wages and labour law (Pakistan).** The HR module (ADR 0030) records who worked and for how long; it does not apply any legal rate. Before payroll (next milestone) is used for real pay, an accountant or labour adviser must confirm, for each province the restaurant is in: (a) the current **minimum wage** and whether daily-wage and piece-rate staff are covered; (b) the **overtime** rule (a multiple of the normal rate? after how many hours a day or week?) and whether the 30-minute threshold used today is acceptable; (c) **annual, casual and sick leave** entitlements and whether unused leave is paid out; (d) **public holidays** worked: extra pay or a day in lieu; (e) **EOBI** and provincial **social security** contributions, who pays and from what wage; (f) **income tax withheld from salaries** (slabs) and who files; (g) **gratuity / notice pay** when someone leaves; (h) whether paying wages through JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank is required above some amount. Until answered, payroll uses only what the owner types (gross pay, allowances, deductions) and keeps these switched off.
47. **Staff privacy.** CNIC, address and emergency contact are personal data shown only to people with `employee.view`. Decide who may see them (the accountant today), how long data of former staff is kept, and whether staff must consent to a PIN clock-in on a shared device.
48. **Leave and payroll rules.** Leave (ADR 0031) counts only working days and stores no legal entitlement. Before payroll uses it, confirm per leave type: the yearly days, whether unused days carry over or are paid out, whether a worked public holiday is paid extra or given back as a day, and whether sick leave needs a medical certificate after some days.
49. **Payroll: what the system does not calculate yet.** Payroll (ADR 0032) computes basic pay, absence, optional overtime and optional lateness deductions from the owner's typed settings and records payments. It does **not** compute: income tax withheld from salary, EOBI / provincial social security (employee and employer shares), minimum-wage checks, paid-holiday premium, notice pay, or gratuity. For each, an accountant must say whether it applies to this restaurant (size, province, staff count) and the exact rate/threshold; until then the owner enters such amounts as manual lines. Also confirm the **month divisor** (flat 30 vs real days) and whether a daily-wage worker must be paid for weekly offs and public holidays. And: should the **payslip** carry the restaurant's NTN/EOBI number, and should salaries be paid through a bank/wallet above some amount?
