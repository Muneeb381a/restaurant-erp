# ADR 0032 — Payroll (HR, milestone 3)

Status: accepted. Builds on ADR 0030 (attendance) and 0031 (leave).

- **Arithmetic is pure and integer** (`packages/domain/src/hr/payroll.ts`, property-tested). Every line is computed with exact BigInt intermediates and rounded **half-up once**. Days are half-day units. Months: salary / divisor per day, where the divisor is a setting (flat 30 by default, or the real month length). Deductions are capped at the salary.
- **A pay run covers one period** (a month, a week or a day) and holds one payslip per person. A monthly salary only fits a monthly run. Pay types: monthly salary, daily wage (paid days x rate; weekly offs/holidays paid only if the owner says so), hourly (worked minutes x rate).
- **Draft, then approved.** A draft is recalculated freely (attendance, leave, holidays, rates and recurring allowances are re-read; manual lines survive). Approval is refused while any working day is unmarked, any net pay is negative, the period has not ended, or the run is empty. Approval needs `payroll.approve` (owner only by default); the manager prepares (`payroll.manage`), the accountant can also pay (`payroll.pay`).
- **Approved means frozen, in the database.** Triggers refuse any change to an approved/cancelled run, its payslips and lines; runs are never deleted. `assertPeriodOpen` also refuses attendance marks, clock-ins, leave approvals/cancellations and holiday changes on days inside an approved run (`PAYROLL_PERIOD_LOCKED`). A mistake is corrected with a **manual line in the next run**, with a reason, audited.
- **Money out goes through the existing ledger.** A salary payment creates an `expenses` row (category `SALARY`, paid from owner cash, bank or the drawer) and an append-only `payroll_payments` row in one transaction, idempotent on `Idempotency-Key`. A trigger refuses a payment above the net pay; part payments are allowed.
- **No legal number is built in.** Overtime is OFF and, when switched on, its multiplier is a typed setting; lateness costs nothing unless "N lates = 1 day" is set; no minimum wage, EOBI, social security, income tax or gratuity is calculated. These are settings under Admin, Payroll, Rules (owner only) and OPEN-QUESTIONS #46/#49. Until an accountant confirms them, extra deductions are typed by hand as manual lines.
- **Payslip**: shown with the arithmetic in words (e.g. "2 day(s) at salary / 30"), printable from the browser, and shareable as a WhatsApp text (the person taps the link; nothing is sent by the server).
